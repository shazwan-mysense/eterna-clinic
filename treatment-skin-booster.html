Skin boosters

水光焕肤

Hydration where creams can't reach — micro-droplets of hyaluronic acid placed just under the skin for a glow that looks like eight hours of sleep.

About this treatment 疗程介绍

The quiet glow treatment

Skin boosters are micro-injections of soft, un-crosslinked hyaluronic acid — the same molecule your skin makes naturally. Rather than changing your features, they change your skin quality: hydration, elasticity, and that lit-from-within luminosity that makeup can only imitate.

At Éterna, every skin booster session begins with a doctor's skin analysis, uses only authentic products opened in front of you, and ends with a personal aftercare plan.

  • Deep, lasting hydration
  • Softer fine lines
  • Refined skin texture
  • A natural, non-filtered glow
Session time45 – 60 minutes, including numbing
DowntimeMinimal — small bumps settle within 24–48 hours
Results appearFrom week two, building over a month
Recommended courseThree sessions, four weeks apart
