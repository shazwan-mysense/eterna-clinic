Skin Boosters

Hydration where creams can't reach — micro-droplets of hyaluronic acid placed just under the skin for a glow that looks like eight hours of sleep.

About Skin Boosters

Skin boosters are micro-injections of soft, un-crosslinked hyaluronic acid — the same molecule your skin makes naturally. Rather than changing your features, they change your skin quality: hydration, elasticity, and that lit-from-within luminosity that makeup can only imitate.

At Éterna, every skin booster session begins with a doctor's skin analysis, uses only authentic products opened in front of you, and ends with a personal aftercare plan. Sessions start from RM 980, with course pricing confirmed at consultation.

How it Works

After numbing cream settles in for 20 minutes, our doctor places micro-droplets of hyaluronic acid across the treatment area with the gentlest technique. A session takes 45–60 minutes, including preparation.

Most guests describe it as small pinches — very manageable, even for first-timers. Tiny bumps at the injection points settle within 24–48 hours; most guests return to normal plans the same day.

Expect the glow to build from week two and deepen over a month. We recommend a course of three sessions, four weeks apart, then maintenance once or twice a year.

Skip make-up for 24 hours, alcohol and intense exercise for 48. We check in personally two days after your session, and your aftercare plan is adjusted to how your skin responds.

Who are skin boosters for?

  • Dry, dull or tired-looking skin.
  • Fine lines from dehydration.
  • Enlarged pores or uneven texture.
  • Skin that "eats" skincare with little to show.
  • Anyone wanting glow without changing features.
